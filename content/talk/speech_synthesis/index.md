## About the course

How does your phone turn your voice into text, and text into a voice? This course opens up the black boxes of automatic speech recognition (ASR) and text-to-speech synthesis (TTS) for linguists. No programming background is assumed: all practical work happens in scaffolded Google Colab notebooks, and the mathematics is built up from ideas phoneticians already use — vowel spaces as vector spaces, spectrograms as functions of waveforms.

Over thirteen weeks we walk the same road the field itself took: template matching, hidden Markov models, neural networks, end-to-end models, and today's foundation models — with stops along the way for forced alignment, speaker recognition, and the ethics of voice technology.
